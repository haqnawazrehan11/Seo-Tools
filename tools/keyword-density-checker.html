What Is Keyword Density?

Keyword density is the percentage of times a particular keyword or phrase appears in a piece of content compared with the total number of words. It can help writers review how frequently a target term is used.

How to Use the Keyword Density Checker

  1. Enter the keyword or phrase you want to check.
  2. Paste your article or content into the text box.
  3. Click Check Keyword Density.
  4. Review the total word count, keyword count, and density percentage.

Why Keyword Density Matters

Keyword frequency can be useful when reviewing content for relevance and consistency. However, there is no single keyword-density percentage that guarantees better search rankings. Content should primarily be useful, readable, natural, and aligned with the searcher's intent.

SEO Tip: Avoid repeating keywords unnaturally. Use related terms and natural language where they genuinely help explain the topic.
