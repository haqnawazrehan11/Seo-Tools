What Is Keyword Density?

Keyword density shows how frequently a keyword appears in your content compared with the total number of words. It can help you understand how often a target keyword is being used on a page.

How to Use This Keyword Density Checker

Enter your target keyword in the keyword box, then paste or type your content into the text area. The tool automatically calculates the total words, keyword occurrences and keyword density percentage.

Why Check Keyword Density?

Checking keyword usage can help you review your content and avoid using a keyword excessively. Always focus on writing useful, natural and readable content for your visitors.
