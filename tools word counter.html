What Does a Word Counter Do?

A word counter helps you quickly measure the length of your content. It can be useful when writing blog posts, articles, essays, product descriptions, social media posts and other online content.

This tool counts words and characters directly in your browser. No account or complicated setup is required.

How to Use This Word Counter

Paste or type your content into the text box. The tool automatically updates the word, character and sentence counts as you type. It also provides an estimated reading time.
